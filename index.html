OJFINEX is a student organization of FEU Manila's Institute of Accounts, Business and Finance. We create ways for aspiring finance executives to learn together, test their ideas, and contribute beyond the classroom.

01

Learn practical finance

Build confidence and financial literacy through workshops, discussions, and hands-on learning.

02

Challenge your thinking

Take part in finance competitions that put analysis, teamwork, and clear decisions to work.

03

Connect with the field

Learn from mentors and industry conversations, and grow your network with fellow students.

04

Make knowledge count

Bring financial education and socially responsible service into the wider community.

Our foundation

Purpose in every step.

We believe finance knowledge is most powerful when it is shared with care, practiced ethically, and used to help people grow.

Our mission

To promote financial literacy and nurture socially responsible students by fostering ethical practices through meaningful outreach and personal growth.

Our vision

To be a competitive academic-business organization and an agent of social change, advancing financial education through proactive student and community involvement.
